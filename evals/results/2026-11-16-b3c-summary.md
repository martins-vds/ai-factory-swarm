## What the baseline shows

- **The Migrators are stable.**
  - All the synthetic Migrators except LL-01 pass by majority, and LL-03, LL-04 and LL-05 pass every run.
  - LL-01's failures are a case problem, not the agent's: see *Case text to fix*.
  - The seed-flush failures are gone (#167, #170, #174).
- **The Verifier is the weak spot.** Only one of the six Verifier cases passes by majority.
  - **It misses real layering defects.** BK-05E and BK-05F fail every run, and LL-02 fails 2 of 3. The live
    Verifier caught BK-05E's and LL-02's defects. BK-05F's two defects were missed live too, and found in one
    earlier eval run.
  - **It raises serious findings on clean work** in LL-03 verify and BK-05G verify, each in 2 of 3 runs.
- **The data and UI cases that run long are fragile.** DM-05D and BK-05G are pilot data and UI tickets: they
  end in handoffs and one-hour timeouts.
