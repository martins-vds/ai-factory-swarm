## BK-05F, at last

Every run covered both ledger rules with a test that calls `CargoRouting.assign_route` directly. Each recorded
`route-specification-satisfied` as `enforced: false` and raised a major finding citing that test:
- **r1:** `assign_route` persists an itinerary that misses the cargo's deadline, and one never issued as a
  candidate.
- **r2:** `assign_route` accepts a stale candidate after the deadline changes, leaving the cargo `MISROUTED`
  instead of refusing it.
