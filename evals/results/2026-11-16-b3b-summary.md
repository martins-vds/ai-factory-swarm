## The bar

- **More than half the cases pass in every run: met.**
- **Every must-pass case gives the same result in every run: not met.** Seven cases varied across the three
  runs.

## Recurring causes

1. **Returns with no mutation score.** This was the most common failure: LL-01 (r1), LL-03 (r1, r3), DM-05D
   (r1, r3) and BK-05G (r2). Gates often passed anyway. The agent finished without recording the score.
2. **Sequences after a database flush.** In LL-03 (r3), the isolation fixture restores the seed rows, but the
   loan-number sequence doesn't continue after them, so a new loan gets `L1` where the test expects `L4`.
3. **BK-05F verify is over-strict on a clean case.** It raised serious findings in all three runs, on work the
   live Verifier passed.
4. **The Verifiers' verdicts vary between runs.** LL-02, LL-04 and BK-05E each failed once in three runs.

## Recommendation

- **Judge must-pass cases by majority of three.** On that rule, LL-01, LL-02 verify, LL-04 verify and BK-05E
  verify pass, and LL-03 and DM-05D fail.
- **A Migrator can't finish without a mutation score.** The finish check refuses a return whose
  `mutation_changed` is empty. A number or a valid `n/a: …` reason is required.
- **The isolation fixture also resets sequences,** or the stack's guidance says to compute IDs from the data,
  never from a fixed sequence position.
- **Review BK-05F verify's findings** against the live Verifier's pass, and decide whether the case or the
  Verifier is wrong.
