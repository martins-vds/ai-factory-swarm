one JUnit class, tier B). The work is graded against the case base on two measures:
- the ledger it wrote, against seven golden rules read from the code and tests;
- the characterization, against five golden values from the source's own test.

**Configurations:**

## Against the decision rule
The plan's rule is to fold only if B is no worse on both runs and the session finished within its lifetime.
**Both are met on this case.**

**Caveats:**
- **The case is small:** a source of four files. The risk a fold carries is a large scope that won't fit one
  session's lifetime or context, and this case can't show that.
- **The second return was missed:** a folded role needs one return contract that the finish check enforces,
  not "two returns" in prose.
- **Not measured:** this case doesn't measure the plan's "prediction matches" metric (observed against
  predicted).
