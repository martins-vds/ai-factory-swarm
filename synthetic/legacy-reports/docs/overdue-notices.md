# Overdue notices

Printed with the monthly report. One notice per member with at least one overdue loan at month end.

- Lists each overdue item with its due date and days overdue at month end.
- Members owing more than $25.00 in late fees get the second-notice wording ("Your borrowing is suspended
  until the balance is paid") instead of the first-notice wording.
- Members with an e-mail address on file were also sent the notice by e-mail from 2016 on.
